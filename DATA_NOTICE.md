# Data notice — Iraqi legislation text

This repository may contain or point to **Iraqi legislation text** (laws,
amendments, related catalog metadata) obtained from public sources such as
official or quasi-official legislation databases.

## Not official

Nothing in this project is an **official** government publication, certified
copy, or authoritative consolidation. Always verify against the competent
Iraqi authorities and gazettes when accuracy matters.

## No warranty

Corpus snapshots, scraped JSONL, sample fixtures, and derived indexes are
provided **as-is**, without warranty of completeness, currency, or correctness.
OCR gaps, missing image-only PDFs, amendment lag, and catalog errors can and
do occur.

## Not legal advice

This toolkit is for **search and drafting aids** — finding and quoting
relevant provisions — **not** legal advice. Output that looks like guidance
is still general information. Consult a qualified lawyer for your situation.

A typical product disclaimer (Arabic):

> معلومات قانونية عامة وليست استشارة قانونية.
